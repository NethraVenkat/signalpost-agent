## Changes and decisions

- Budget: one DNS nomination for a www/non-www pair; cached checks cost no additional nomination. Source vetoes precede work, and in-run responses/errors are reused across companies under a URL/permission-scope lock. Identity is independently proved for each company. Alternate hosts require DNS/connect failure; HTTP errors and source vetoes cannot trigger them.
- The final reuse step also locks completed candidate checks across www/non-www spellings under the same path/permission scope. Responses and non-budget source failures are reused, while company identity verdicts are always recomputed. A regression test explicitly rejects the second company when the shared body proves only the first.
- Candidate order: registry homepage, registry email domain, then the strongest full-name variants before subunit/brand and remaining spelling nominations. Tests explicitly check registry and full-name precedence. Exact-number proof immediately stops further candidate/proof-page work.
- The separate 8/13 cap experiment was required because failures remained above Stage 3. Its HTTP cost/runtime and section states are shown separately; no cap above 8/13 was used. The ordering step retested 6/9 and reached six websites but remained at 26 website / 33 activity failures. The final comparison therefore uses explicit `--website-request-cap 8 --website-total-request-cap 13` flags; CLI/library defaults stay 6/9.
- The conditional fallback reversion was not needed after recovery to the Stage 3 failure targets.
- Numeric LinkedIn `/company/<digits>` links are accepted only in the verified site's own header/footer, with group, founder, agency, hidden and article contexts excluded. Named profiles still require exact-name handle agreement. The numeric exception has a distinct proof tier and retains website identity, registry name and verbatim link evidence. No platform page is fetched.
- The public-activity allowance is bounded by an absolute eight website HTTP attempts. The resource audit checks actual attempts and DNS nominations for every company in every measured run; full per-company counts are in [resource_audit.json](resource_audit.json).
- Both withheld links and every website/social URL addition/removal are listed with organisation number, company name, URL, proof tier and exact excerpts in [new_websites.md](new_websites.md). Full locators, hashes and link context are in [url_audit.json](url_audit.json).
- Fresh acquisition is subject to live source variation. No incomplete result is relabelled as checked absence. Section failures and partial available activity claims are reported independently.
