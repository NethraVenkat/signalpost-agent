## Our issues to address before recall

- Candidate precheck runs on only the original hostname before www/non-www fallback, so NXDOMAIN on one hostname can prevent checking the other. Fix without changing the DNS/request ceilings.
- The collector continues scheduling proof pages after the remaining budget cannot cover them, then schedules more candidates; it also spends spare slots on sitemap/RSS discovery. Remove sitemap/RSS candidate discovery and reserve bounded slots for linked proof pages; do not raise budgets or turn incomplete checks into not_available.
- Linked PDF/privacy documents can consume slots despite being outside the HTML extractor. Filter known non-HTML file suffixes before requests.
- Existing name/address HIGH rules can admit group sites without an exact number. Add a group veto; phone/email shared by management providers cannot override it.
- DNS/TLS/timeouts, robots and foreign-entity evidence remain explicit failures/vetoes when not demonstrably our bug. No source outage is assumed from transport logs alone.
