# G2: consolidated statements; group-tree access pending

The [first-party Regnskapsregisteret OpenAPI](https://data.brreg.no/regnskapsregisteret/regnskap/v3/api-docs)
explicitly distinguishes `SELSKAP` and `KONSERN` and identifies the filing ID,
organisation number, accounting period and currency. The existing approved
`https://data.brreg.no/regnskapsregisteret/regnskap/{orgnr}` response already
contains group statements; this change sends no new requests or query variants.
Brreg's [dataset licence statement](https://www.brreg.no/bruke-data-fra-bronnoysundregistrene/datasett-og-api/)
identifies NLOD. Source provenance is retained in every claim.

Group figures publish only under `accounts.consolidated.{period_end}.{filing_id}.*`,
with `account_type: KONSERN` and filing ID in reporting metadata. Exact filing
owner and valid date boundaries are mandatory. Malformed or mismatched group
records are withheld independently of entity figures. Company ratios and the
company-only timeline do not consume group statements. Profiles explicitly say
Consolidated group and show filing IDs. Both output and accuracy auditors enforce
that namespace, filing ID, account type, exact orgnr and source pointer.

The real fixture is an unchanged official response saved by **our own** baseline
run at `45afc2b`, for organisation `991835423`. Its source URL, retrieval time
and original SHA-256 are committed beside the fixture. It includes both company
and group statements, with group filing IDs `6290461` and `7105229`. No competitor
code, response or bundled data was used.

The separately documented [group-tree endpoint](https://data.brreg.no/enhetsregisteret/api/dokumentasjon/no/),
`/enhetsregisteret/api/konsernstruktur/{orgnr}`, is not enabled: canonical origin
robots remained unavailable after the bounded retries in [G1](G1.md).
A parent-company boolean in an accounts response is not a named parent/owner
relationship, and no such relationship is inferred. This completes only the
consolidated-account part of G2; group relationships remain pending.

An offline replay of our 100 saved baseline responses (not a live run) yields
180 available consolidated line claims from seven filings at four companies.
[G2-replay.json](G2-replay.json) records the org numbers and filing IDs.

Regression results: 1,417 tests passed in 1,030.14 seconds; the final source/site
focused check passed 34 tests in 14.27 seconds. [Output](G2-tests.txt).
