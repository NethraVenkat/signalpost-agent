# Patentstyret research only

Checked 18 October 2026 (IST). No integration or account created.

The official [access guide](https://developer.patentstyret.no/docs-get-access) requires registration and an API subscription key. This conflicts with the unattended run's prohibition on personal-account credentials, so no runtime client was added.

The [official data portal](https://developer.patentstyret.no/) says many Norwegian business right holders can be identified by organisation number and describes company-portfolio and trademark-detail endpoints. Not every right necessarily has a usable Norwegian owner number. Its data is dynamic.

The [government catalogue](https://data.norge.no/en/data-services/4fa0433f-2fa8-3235-8c8b-f0a577e25c48/the-registry-for-patents-designs-and-trademarks-in-norway) declares NLOD 2.0. An attributed, dated offline nomination index appears feasible in principle (inference), subject to confirming the selected fields, redistribution conditions and acquisition limits. Such an index would provide brand candidates only; publication would still require exact website org-number proof. This is not a verified bulk-download mechanism or a licence clearance for every associated image.
